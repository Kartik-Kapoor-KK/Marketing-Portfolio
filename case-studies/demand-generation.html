Case Study / Demand Generation

Turning A Webinar Into A Multi-Channel Demand Campaign.

An integrated campaign combining paid media, email, LinkedIn, and organic promotion that increased webinar registrations by 37%.

37% Webinar Registration Growth

The Situation

One Event. Multiple Acquisition Channels.

The objective was to increase webinar registrations while reaching relevant professional audiences.

Instead of relying on one acquisition channel, the campaign connected paid media, email marketing, LinkedIn activity, and organic promotion.

This allowed the webinar to function as a demand generation asset rather than simply an isolated event.

Campaign Architecture

01

Paid Acquisition

Used LinkedIn Ads and Google Ads to reach relevant professional audiences.

02

Email Distribution

Used email campaigns to activate existing contacts and drive registrations.

03

Organic Promotion

Supported paid acquisition with LinkedIn content and organic distribution.

Marketing Thinking

The Event Was The Asset. Distribution Was The Engine.

The key principle was to treat the webinar as content that could be distributed through several channels rather than as a one-time event.

Paid campaigns provided reach. Email provided direct access to existing audiences. LinkedIn provided organic visibility and engagement.

The Result

37%

Increase In Webinar Registrations.

The campaign increased webinar registrations by connecting multiple acquisition channels around one clear marketing objective.
