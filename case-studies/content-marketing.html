Case Study / Content Marketing

Building A Content Engine Around Product And Buyer Problems.

A broad product-led content portfolio covering blogs, eBooks, newsletters, brochures, landing pages, webinars, and thought leadership.

50+ Content Assets

The Situation

Content Needed To Do More Than Fill A Calendar.

The objective was to build content that could educate prospects, communicate product value, support demand generation, and help sales conversations.

That meant creating different content formats for different stages of the buyer journey.

The portfolio therefore included both educational and product-focused resources.

Content Architecture

01

Educational

Blogs and thought leadership around industry and buyer problems.

02

Long-Form

eBooks and deeper resources supporting consideration.

03

Product

Product-led content connecting capabilities with business value.

04

Campaign

Landing pages, newsletters, webinars, and campaign assets.

Marketing Thinking

Start With The Buyer. Choose The Format Second.

The content format should follow the audience's question, not the other way around.

Awareness-stage audiences need useful education. Prospects evaluating solutions need deeper resources. Sales conversations need concise product and value communication.

The Result

50+

Content Assets Built Across The Marketing Journey.

The content portfolio created a reusable foundation for organic visibility, campaigns, thought leadership, product communication, and sales support.
