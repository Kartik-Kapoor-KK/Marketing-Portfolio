01

The Challenge

Features Explain What A Product Does. Positioning Explains Why.

HR technology can quickly become a crowded category where products begin to sound remarkably similar.

The challenge was to communicate capabilities in a way that connected them to real business problems and meaningful outcomes.

That required thinking about the product from the market's perspective, not only from the product's perspective.

02 / The Positioning Lens

Product. Problem. Proof.

The messaging became stronger when these three elements were considered together.

04 / Go-To-Market

Positioning Only Matters When It Travels.

Product marketing sits between the product, the market and the teams responsible for bringing the story to customers.

PRODUCT Understand

Capabilities and differentiation.

MARKETING Translate

Positioning, messaging and content.

SALES Activate

Sales narratives and enablement.

MARKET Validate

Response, feedback and learning.

05 / The Takeaway

The Job Is Not To Describe The Product.

The job is to make the product easier for the market to understand, remember and believe.

That means connecting what the product does with why the audience should care, then carrying that message consistently through the buyer journey.

That is where product marketing becomes more than messaging. It becomes a bridge between product and market.
