Case Study / Growth Marketing

Turning LinkedIn Into A Growth Channel.

How I approached audience growth by treating LinkedIn as a strategic distribution channel, rather than simply a social media platform.

300% Audience Growth
Company ClayHR
Category HR Technology
Focus Organic Growth
Outcome 300% Growth

The Challenge

A Company Page Should Not Be The Strategy.

The challenge was not simply increasing the number of followers. The bigger question was how to make LinkedIn useful as a channel for visibility, credibility and ongoing audience development.

That meant moving away from a purely company-centric publishing approach and thinking about what people actually wanted to consume, share and discuss.

The Approach

01

Understand

Identify the topics, audiences and conversations where the brand had a legitimate reason to participate.

02

Reframe

Turn product and industry knowledge into content that was easier for an audience to consume.

03

Distribute

Build consistency around publishing, formats and audience interaction.

04

Measure

Track audience development and identify what earned attention.

300%

The Result

Growth Became Evidence.

The work contributed to a 300% increase in LinkedIn audience growth, turning the channel into a more meaningful part of the brand's marketing presence.

What I Took From It

Audience Growth Is A Marketing Outcome.

The strongest social growth does not come from chasing reach. It comes from understanding what an audience values and building a repeatable system around it.
