Content That Has A Job.

Building content around the questions, problems and decisions that matter to an HR technology audience.

01

The Starting Point

More Content Does Not Automatically Create More Demand.

The real opportunity was to make content useful enough to earn attention before asking the audience to buy anything.

For an HR technology company, the audience is constantly dealing with questions around people, processes, technology and business outcomes.

Those questions became the starting point for deciding what deserved to be created.

02 / The Content System

One Topic. Many Useful Expressions.

Instead of treating every asset as an isolated piece of content, I approached content as an ecosystem.

CORE Audience Problem
INSIGHT Article

Build understanding.

DISTRIBUTION Social

Earn attention.

DEPTH Webinar

Explore the topic.

AUTHORITY Publication

Extend credibility.

03 / Give Every Asset A Job

Content Should Move Something.

DISCOVER Help Someone Find The Brand

Search-led and socially distributed content created an entry point into the brand.

UNDERSTAND Make A Complex Topic Clear

Educational content helped audiences understand a problem before considering a solution.

TRUST Demonstrate Expertise

Thought leadership and external publications helped strengthen credibility.

ACT Create A Next Step

Strong content should naturally connect the audience to another useful action.

04 / Earned Visibility

Content Should Travel Beyond Your Own Channels.

Part of the content work also extended into external publications, giving the brand opportunities to reach audiences outside its owned ecosystem.

INDUSTRY WORLD HRMS & Talent Management Read Publication ↗ BUSINESS VIEWPOINT AI-Powered HRMS Read Publication ↗ THE ENTERPRISE WORLD Next-Generation HR Software Read Publication ↗ THE ENTERPRISE WORLD Setting New Perspectives Read Publication ↗

05 / The Takeaway

The Best Content Earns Its Place.

Every piece should answer a question, remove friction, create curiosity or strengthen trust.

That is the difference between producing content and building a content marketing system.

The objective is not volume. It is relevance multiplied by consistency.
