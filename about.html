About Kartik

I Like Making Complex Things Make Sense.

I am a B2B technology marketer working across product marketing, go-to-market, demand generation and content.

My experience has taken me from understanding customers and products to building the stories, campaigns and systems that connect those products with the market.

The Throughline

Curiosity About Why People Buy.

I have always been interested in the space between what a product does and what a buyer actually understands.

That is where much of my marketing work lives. I look at a product, the people it is built for, the problem it solves and the conversation already happening in the market.

Then I work backwards into positioning, messaging, content, campaigns and distribution.

How I Think About Marketing

Start With The Problem.

01

Understand Before You Communicate

The better I understand the buyer, the product and the context, the less marketing needs to rely on generic language.

02

Make Complexity Useful

Technical products do not need to be made simplistic. They need to be made understandable in terms the audience actually cares about.

03

Connect Strategy To Execution

A strategy is only useful when it can become messaging, content, campaigns, workflows and measurable action.

04

Let Evidence Change The Plan

Performance is not just a scorecard. It is feedback that should influence what happens next.
