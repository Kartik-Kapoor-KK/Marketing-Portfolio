CASE STUDY / DEMAND GENERATION

Webinar Demand Generation With 37% Registration Growth.

A multi-channel demand generation approach combining paid media, email, LinkedIn, and organic promotion.

37% Webinar Registration Growth

The Challenge

Increase Webinar Registrations Through Multiple Channels.

The objective was to increase registrations for webinar campaigns while reaching relevant professional audiences.

Rather than relying on a single acquisition channel, the campaign used a combination of paid media, email marketing, LinkedIn activity, and organic promotion.

The Campaign

01

Paid Media

Used LinkedIn Ads and Google Ads to reach relevant professional audiences and drive registrations.

02

Email Marketing

Used targeted email communication to promote the event and encourage registrations.

03

Organic Promotion

Supported paid campaigns with LinkedIn content and organic distribution.

The Result

37%

Growth In Webinar Registrations.

The multi-channel campaign increased webinar registrations by combining paid acquisition with owned and organic marketing channels.
