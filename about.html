About

I Like Marketing That Solves A Real Business Problem.

My Story

Understanding The Business Comes First.

My career has moved from business development and product understanding into B2B technology marketing.

That progression has shaped how I approach marketing. I do not see marketing as simply producing content or running campaigns. I see it as the discipline of understanding a business problem and making the product easier to understand, trust, and buy.

At ClayHR, I developed experience across HR Tech, B2B SaaS, product marketing, content, demand generation, paid campaigns, webinars, websites, SEO/GEO, and sales enablement.

At AI-Priori, I am applying that experience to Industrial AI, where the challenge is even more pronounced because the underlying technology and operational problems can be highly technical.

How I Work

Clear Thinking. Useful Content. Measurable Outcomes.

I start with the problem before deciding what the marketing output should be.

What is the customer struggling with? Who owns that problem? What does the business need to change? What does the product actually solve? What evidence can we use?

From there, I work backwards into positioning, messaging, content, campaigns, distribution, and measurement.

The goal is not to make marketing look busy. The goal is to make marketing useful.
